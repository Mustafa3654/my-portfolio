Wassili

Multi-vendor delivery & marketplace platform

Problem

Local shops took orders in WhatsApp threads and dispatched drivers by voice note. Nothing was recorded, prices drifted between USD and LBP, and nobody could say where an order was without calling three people. Off-the-shelf delivery apps assumed a single vendor, one currency, and a customer willing to install something.

Solution

A cart that splits one basket across several merchants, then hands each merchant its own slice as a formatted WhatsApp message they can accept without leaving the app they already live in. Every order gets a record, a dual-currency total pinned to the day's rate, and a tracking state that customers and drivers read from the same source.

  • One cart splits into per-merchant orders
  • WhatsApp dispatch, no merchant app required
  • USD + LBP pricing on a managed rate
  • Driver assignment and live order state

Sortifya

Crowdsourced micro-task data platform

Problem

Businesses sit on archives of scanned paper that OCR mangles — handwriting, stamps, Arabic and English on the same page. Digitising it means paying people, and paying people means tracking who did which page, whether it was right, and what they are owed. That bookkeeping is what usually kills the project.

Solution

A queue where workers claim a single PDF, transcribe it into a structured sheet, and submit for review. Approvals credit a per-worker balance; withdrawal requests push a notification straight into the admin panel with the payout amount and the work it covers. The ledger is the product — the transcription is just the input.

  • PDF extraction into structured Excel
  • Task claiming with per-worker locks
  • Review queue before anything is paid
  • Withdrawal alerts in the admin panel

Practice

What building here actually requires.

Not a wall of logos. These are the constraints that decide whether a system survives contact with a real business.

Dual currency

Prices that hold when the rate moves

Totals stored in USD, shown in LBP at a rate the owner controls, and frozen onto the order at checkout so a receipt never disagrees with itself later.

WhatsApp-first

Meeting people where they already are

Structured orders delivered as readable messages. No merchant downloads an app, and the system still keeps a clean record of what was agreed.

Arabic & RTL

Bilingual as a layout problem

Right-to-left support with mirrored components, correct numeral handling and translated admin panels — built in from the schema up, not bolted on.

Admin panels

Software the owner can run alone

Filament back offices with the resources, filters and bulk actions a non-technical owner needs. If they have to call me to change a price, I built it wrong.

Hosting

Deployment is part of the job

Subdomain routing, TLS, cron, backups and restores. Running IT and hosting at ComPutroniX means shipping doesn't stop at the merge.

Thin connections

Fast on the network people have

Server-rendered pages, small payloads and cached queries — because the customer is on mobile data during a power cut, not on fibre.
